Privacy Policy for Hunting Hours

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information Hunting Hours ("the app") collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. The app is developed and published by Elizabeth Bornhoeft ("we," "us," or "our").

Information we collect

Hunting Hours is designed to work without requiring an account, tracking, or advertising. The information it handles is limited to the data you enter into the app to set your hunting dates and alarms:

  • Hunting dates and hours you add — the dates, start/end hunting hours, and alarm times you create in the app.
  • Seasons — any named groups (e.g. "Deer season") you create to organize your dates, along with their colors.
  • App preferences — settings such as your chosen vibration pattern and which alarm toggles are enabled.

We do not collect your name, contact details, precise location, device identifiers, or any analytics or usage data. The app does not use your device's location, and it contains no advertising or analytics software development kits (SDKs).

How your information is stored and synced

The data listed above is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced privately through Apple's CloudKit service to your own iCloud account so it is available across your devices. This sync is handled entirely by Apple's iCloud infrastructure under Apple's own privacy terms; we do not operate any servers of our own and do not have access to this data.

Sharing a season with another person

If you choose to share a season with someone else using the app's sharing feature, the dates and hunting hours in that season are shared with the Apple ID(s) you invite, via Apple's iCloud sharing (CKShare). This only happens if you actively start a share and choose who to invite — it is never automatic.

Notifications

Hunting Hours asks for permission to send local notifications so it can alert you at the alarm times you set. These are scheduled entirely on your device; we do not send push notifications and have no server that contacts you.

Data sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. The only data transmission involved is your own private iCloud sync (and, if you choose to use it, iCloud sharing with people you personally invite), both of which are handled by Apple.

Your choices

  • You can delete any date, season, or your entire alarm history at any time from within the app.
  • You can stop iCloud sync for the app at any time in your device's Settings.
  • You can disable notification permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

Children's privacy

Hunting Hours is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
